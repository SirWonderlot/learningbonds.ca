Canada Learning Bond

Up to $2,000 from the Government of Canada for your child's education after high school.

It is free to use this service, and free to apply for the Bond.

  • $2,000for each eligible child
  • $0your family does not have to put in any money
  • Past yearsmissed years can still be collected

How it works

  1. Check eligibilityAnswer a few questions. It takes about a minute.
  2. See your amountIncluding past years that have not been collected.
  3. Speak with a specialistA licensed Learning Bond Specialist helps you apply.
  4. CollectThe Bond is deposited into your child's RESP.
More about the Canada Learning Bond
  • Amount: up to $2,000 per child. $500 is paid for the first year a child is eligible, then $100 for each year after, up to age 15.
  • Opening costs: the government also pays $25 into the RESP with the first $500, to help cover the cost of opening it.
  • Past years: amounts from past eligible years can still be collected.
  • Income limit (July 2026 to June 2027): adjusted family net income of $58,523 or less for a family with one to three children. The limit is higher for larger families.
  • Total payments: over 2.3 million children have received the Bond, totalling more than $2.4 billion since 2004.
  • New recipients: 174,941 children received the Bond for the first time in 2025.
  • Source: Canada Education Savings Program: 2025 Annual Statistical Review, figures as of the end of 2025.
