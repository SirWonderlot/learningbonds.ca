Canada Learning Bond: Eligibility

Is your child eligible?

Answer a few questions to see how much your child could receive, including amounts from past years.

Residents of some provinces are entitled to provincial government entitlements to help fund their child's future post-secondary education.

The income limit for the Canada Learning Bond depends on the number of children in your family.

Do you have any children aged 18, 19 or 20?

Young adults aged 18 to 20 can apply for the Canada Learning Bond themselves until their 21st birthday.

The Canada Learning Bond is available to eligible children and young adults under the age of 21.

What is your family net income?

Eligibility for the Canada Learning Bond depends on your family's net income.

You can find your net income on line 23600 of your tax return. If you have a spouse or common-law partner, their net income must also be included.

Does your child currently have a Registered Education Savings Plan (RESP)?

Knowing whether your child already has an RESP helps us determine the appropriate next steps.
